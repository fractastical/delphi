LeanBio: claims, grounded and ranked for Lean

Claims distilled from the literature become nanopubs. Each one is grounded to ontology identifiers, typed as a subject–relation–object statement, and given a belief score from its independent evidence. The best-supported claims that have a mathematical form go to a queue, and from there to a Lean 4 statement in the law registry. A person accepts a law; the pipeline never does.

Belief scores

Belief = E × (1 − R), with E from supporting and R from contradicting evidence in INDRA's SimpleScorer form, each source counted once. The parameters are set by hand and not fitted, so treat belief as an order, not a probability. Most claims have only two elicited model opinions, which is why so many share one value.

How a score is made

P_wrong(s) = min(1, syst_s + rand_s ^ n_s)
E = 1 - PROD P_wrong   (supporting)
R = 1 - PROD P_wrong   (contradicting)
belief = E * (1 - R)

Laws by status

Statement types

INDRA's molecular types fire only when a gene, protein or chemical is grounded on both sides; the rest are our law types. Typing is regex rules with unmeasured precision.

Grounding coverage

Gilda (INDRA's grounder) reads each claim; MyGene.info, OLS4 and the repo's taxon inventory cover zebrafish genes and taxa that Gilda does not index, and check identifiers that drafts declare. Low-scoring synonym hits are rejected; near-ties are flagged as ambiguous and never resolved automatically.

The formalization queue

Records with belief ≥ 0.5, a relation that has a mathematical form, no law in the registry yet, and no reviewer decision against them. Theorem-shaped relations come first, then belief. Being on the queue means “worth a look”, not “formalizable”: a reviewer often finds there is nothing to state.

How to contribute

Propose a LeanBio law

  1. A sourced nanopub draft or claim in the repo, with resolvable identifiers.
  2. Grounded entities and a belief at or above 0.5 (node pipeline/leanbio/run.mjs).
  3. A Lean statement with named hypotheses: no axiom, no sorry, a non-vacuity witness, a scope.
  4. node lean/registry/propose.mjs law.lean --meta meta.json --write: builds, checks axioms, rejects statement mutants, checks joint consistency.
  5. node lean/registry/check-exposed.mjs green.
  6. A person accepts it, by role, in a new lawset.

Full criteria: CONTRIBUTING-LEANBIO.md. Open the PR with the leanbio.md template; CI runs the checks.

Harvest nanopubs and query

Read-only SPARQL on the public Knowledge Pixels query service: node pipeline/leanbio/harvest.mjs --count. A harvested nanopub is a candidate; it enters the pipeline as a claim or draft that cites it.

Agents: MCP tools leanbio_queue, leanbio_ground (text to ontology ids), leanbio_claim (claim to nanopub draft, belief and linked laws), once the hub registers them.

Nothing is ever signed or published from a pull request. The held-out exam clade is excluded everywhere.

Every number on this page is read from leanbio/data.json, written by node pipeline/leanbio/run.mjs.
