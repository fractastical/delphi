**The new fail is N2 (no fish below teleost depth limits), and no amendment applies to it.**
`scenarios/pressure.mjs` (changed 23:06 local) now returns `survives: null` and `feasible: null` for
zebrafish-adult, zebrafish-larva and winged-zebrafish at 9,000 m and 10,000 m. The stated reason: "the

pre-registered rule says survives must be false, and the observation it rests on covers all fish: no fish of
any kind has been found below 8,400 m (Yancey 2014). A freshwater fish at 10 km is a design request outside
teleost limits, and the studio withholds the verdict. The flying fish and the marine designs still die.

## The one honest sentence (at 03:02 UTC)

**After the fixes, the studio fails nothing under the amended reading. But it passes nothing about the wild
type on its own strength either: every numeric wild-type value that lands in band is now read straight from
the paper it is scored against. That is honest, and most modules now label these values `calibrated`
themselves, but it is not validation. The negative and optimizer controls all hold.**

## Source audit

Code literals only: 162 lines carry constants across 11 modules. 29 have a URL/DOI/PMID within three lines,
90 cite by author-year or repository file only, and 43 have neither. Most of the unsourced ones are defaults
and validity bounds.

Spot checks by real GET of the cited paper for the quoted phrase gave 10 confirmed, 1 unverifiable (D8) and
1 not fetchable through Europe PMC/Crossref (Makiguchi 2013's "50 m"; the physics thread read it from the
publisher PDF). The confirmed claims are:
- Widrick 2018 (15.5 SL/s; Umax 15 % above Ucrit);
- McKee & McHenry 2020 (10.3–15.8°, 740–780 ms);
- Schuster 2023 (11 BL/s, 24 BL/s);
- Choudhary 2026 (19.6 %, 83.9 %);
- Ripley 2022 (Q10 1.39, 1.15);
- Yancey 2014 (261, 991, 8,200 m);
- Lauridsen 2022 (5 % bladder, 5 % denser);
- Durnford 2026 (58.48 ml/min/kg).

No mis-sourced constant was found among those checked.

## What this does not establish

The bands are wide on purpose, because the targets disagree among themselves. A pass is a sanity check,
not validation. The real out-of-sample evidence the studio has is in some modules' own `validatedAgainst`
entries, for example temperature against Morgan 2018 (40.97 °C measured, 41.2 predicted) and Andreassen
2022. This harness only points to those. The genotype side, the morphogen tool and the UI were not tested.
No Aves data was used.
