# Validating designs: results on the four named designs

`sim-engine/validate/` is product 3. For a design it reports:

- the closest real relative, by phenotype and by genotype;
- sanity checks on genotype and phenotype;
- the Lean verdicts, grouped by component;
- a design score.

The score is the subject of a separate, pre-registered validation: see `SCORER-PREREGISTRATION.md` and
`SCORER-RESULTS.md`. Regenerate the tables below with `node sim-engine/validate/run-cases.mjs`; the data are in
`results.json`.

## The honest summary

**The validator separates real organisms from some of our designs, for reasons that can be traced.** It does so
only on the handful of features it reads.

- **The unedited zebrafish reports itself as its own nearest relative, at distance 0**, as required.
- **The winged zebrafish looks exactly like a zebrafish** in the body block. On pectoral fin length it sits between
  the zebrafish and the gliding halfbeaks (nearest: *Oxyporhamphus micropterus*, a real gliding halfbeak). Its two
  gene edits are real alleles (lat4a nr21 T200K; the kcnh2a longfin inversion), and both pass every sequence check.
  The T200K site is a conserved column: no natural orthologue in the panel carries K there. The allele exists only
  as the Daane 2021 lab line.
- **TFF scores implausible (0.35)** for two traced reasons:
  - Its pectoral area, pectoral span and caudal lobes lie outside every real fin reference.
  - The flight muscle it asks for does not fit its body. The mesh fits only 29.5 % of it.

  Its body is the flying fish's, so the body block alone would pass it.
- **The 50:50 chimera scores plausible (0.82).** Its body's nearest real relative is a lenok, *Brachymystax lenok*
  (Salmonidae), at a typical held-out distance. Its flight-muscle budget is 75 % met, which fails the sanity check
  at a 95 % cut but costs the score only P = 0.75. **A blend that is no animal scores plausible:** the scorer
  checks shapes, not whether a shape could be bred. See the hard caveat in `lib/organism.mjs`.
- **Lean decides nothing for any of the four designs.** Every law comes back either cannot-evaluate (the laws
  compare scaling exponents against one reference specimen, at a length that differs from it) or out-of-scope
  (zebrafish and blends). The single verdict that evaluates is G1 for the lat4a T200K edit, and it holds.

## How it works, and what it does not read

**Phenotype.** The design's STATE is embedded in two blocks.

- **Body block:** log SL, log mass/SL³, log depth/SL, log width/SL, and the log peduncle fractions.
  - References: 16 377 FishShapes v1 teleost specimens (measured; no birds exist in that dataset, and an explicit
    filter runs anyway), our 3 real organisms, and every ontogeny stage.
  - Distance is the RMS of robust z-scores.
  - The OOD threshold is the 99th percentile of nearest-neighbour distances of held-out-species specimens to the
    train pool: **0.544**, from n = 3 279 held-out specimens of 1 212 species.
- **Pectoral fin length/SL:**
  - References: 9 Daane 2021 sheet A beloniform species plus our organisms.
  - Threshold: 0.508, by leave-one-group-out (n = 27).
- **The other seven fin features** have only our own two lineages as references. They get an *uncalibrated*
  envelope rule and are reported as such.

**Genotype.** Each gene edit is placed on the MAFFT alignment from `sequences/alignments/`. For each edit the
validator checks:

- whether the reference residue at that position matches the edit;
- identity to every orthologue row;
- the closest species, among rows aligned over at least 60 % of the protein;
- whether the edited residue occurs in any natural orthologue or known allele (`genotype/interventions.json` and
  the alignment's variant list);
- column conservation;
- UniProt domain, transmembrane and region features, mapped only when the lengths match;
- for nucleotide edits, frame and premature stop, using the Ensembl CDS.

Genes outside `sequences/` fall back to NCBI BLAST (`blast:true`; polite, cached, `NOT Aves[Organism]`). The BLAST
path is implemented but was **not exercised** in these results. Annotations come from `genotype/`: the causal chain
and its weakest link, cross-gene caveats, interventions, pathway terms, and the flying-fish orthologue status
(including the two NOT-an-orthologue calls). All of this is a sequence STATE; nothing predicts a phenotype.

**Sanity checks.**

- **Allometry:** each body feature is checked against the range observed across all FishShapes specimens.
- **Mass fits body:** the fill factor, mass / (ρ_water · SL · depth · width), is compared with the FishShapes
  distribution.
- **Organ fit:** two sources feed it.
  - The flight-muscle budget. For unedited repository designs this is the mesh's own fit fraction. Otherwise it is
    analytic: a capacity per body envelope, taken from the tff and chimera meshes where the muscle was clipped.
  - partedit containment results, if the browser supplies them. Containment needs the mesh, so Node cannot run it.
- **Stage consistency:** SL against the growth track at that age, and the swim bladder against its inflation
  milestone.

**Not read, so it cannot lower a score:**

- ray and vertebra counts;
- gill area, haemoglobin P50, red-muscle fraction, body density;
- head length;
- internal organs, apart from the flight-muscle budget;
- physiology, development and behaviour.

The adversarial search in `SCORER-RESULTS.md` finds designs that exploit exactly these.

## Tools

The MCP tools live in `sim-engine/tools/validate-tools.mjs`, with the registration patch in
`tools/VALIDATE-REGISTER.md`. They are:

- `closest_relative_phenotype`
- `closest_relative_genotype`
- `sanity_check_design`
- `lean_by_component`
- `design_score`
- `validate_design` (with `swarm:true`)
- `validate_swarm_tasks`

`http.mjs` already serves all seven at `POST /tool/<name>`. The UI is
`dashboard/public/blueprint3d/validate-panel.js`; the hub's patch to add the Validate button to `perturb.js` is
`dashboard/public/blueprint3d/VALIDATE-PANEL-PATCH.md`.

**Scorer versions.**
- **v1.0.0** is the default. It is pre-registered and validated, and its weights are in `score-weights.json`. The
  tables above use it.
- **v1.1.0** is in `score-weights.v1.1.0.json` (pre-registration `SCORER-PREREGISTRATION-v1.1.0.md`). It adds:
  - STATE-field envelopes;
  - a missing-feature penalty;
  - larval envelopes;
  - tighter fin envelopes.

  Select it with `designScore(design, {version: '1.1.0'})`. The two versions are compared side by side in
  `SCORER-RESULTS.md`.

Run the tests with `node sim-engine/validate/test/validate.mjs`: 24 checks. `MUTATE=<check>` perturbs one check's
input, and each check was seen to fail under its mutation.
