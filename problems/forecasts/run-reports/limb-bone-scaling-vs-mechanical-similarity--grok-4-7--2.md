# Lean derivation of the three similarity exponents

Outcome: completed. The module proves, from explicit positive-mass circular-section
diameter power laws, the geometric, elastic, and stress-similarity area/circumference
exponents. It also proves that the three area exponents are pairwise distinct.

The posture theorem derives required area proportional to `mass^(1 − γ)` when body force
is proportional to mass, effective mechanical advantage is `e mass^γ`, and bone stress is
held constant. The approach needed positive mass and nonzero advantage/stress coefficients;
the module states these hypotheses explicitly. McMahon's separate length exponent `1/4`
is not used to derive diameter, area, or circumference and was therefore not added as a
decorative hypothesis.

The formal result supplies the preregistered exponent targets but does not choose among
them empirically, so it does not settle the open biological problem. Claim
`thompson1917-ch02-03` receives a faithful conditional formalization;
`thompson1917-ch16-04` is marked not-formalized because it concerns wall thickness along
one shaft rather than cross-species allometry.
