# Twelve pentagons are a topological count

Outcome: completed. From Euler's equation, trivalent vertex handshaking, and
pentagon/hexagon face handshaking, the Lean module proves that a spherical lattice made
only of pentagons and hexagons has exactly twelve pentagons. A second theorem includes an
arbitrary material parameter and proves the same count without using it.

This is conditional material-independence of one topological integer only. It does not
prove 120-degree angles, regularity, vesicle templating, mineral-independent morphology,
or that radiolarian shells satisfy the trivalent pentagon/hexagon hypotheses. Consequently
claims `thompson1917-ch09-07`, `thompson1917-ch09-14`, and `thompson1917-ch09-15` are all
marked not-formalized rather than attaching a stub proof to an empirical claim.

The formalization completes the proposed mathematical approach but does not settle the
open biological problem.
