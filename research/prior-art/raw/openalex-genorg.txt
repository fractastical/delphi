This review article identifies and links wellknown AI concepts and algorithms to current solutions in cancer gene therapy. The text documents the whole process in which the genome was characterized and its evolution (as a form of evolutionary algorithm) was studied to understand the optimization mechanism for the pullulan yield. The manuscript narrates evolutionary algorithms within cells, but from a purely biological and genomic perspective, adding an interesting view on in-cell AI.We hope that the topic fulfilled its goal in attracting interest and bringing to the attention of researchers from all fields related to AI, synthetic biology, and genomics that in-cell AI implementation is a diOicult endeavor, but entails great opportunities in its applications.
