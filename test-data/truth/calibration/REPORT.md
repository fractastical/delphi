[historical-verdicts.json](historical-verdicts.json). Each assessor scored every claim twice: **blind** (modern
restatement only) and **attributed** (thinker, work, label and quote, as in the main truth pipeline).
Brier score is the mean squared error of p_true against the verdict: lower is better, 0.25 is answering 0.5
to everything. Accuracy counts p_true > 0.5 as a "true" call. Generated by `score.mjs`.

## Answer-key revisions

Verdicts changed or claims excluded after reviewing assessor rationales. The key was revised after the
first run, so these claims favour the assessors whose objections prompted the change.

- `aristotle-ha06-21`: excluded. The erythrinus is usually identified as Pagellus erythrinus, a protogynous hermaphrodite whose larger fish are male, so the claim fails for it unless the identification is wrong; the channa part holds.
- `aristotle-ga02-19`: Verdict changed from true after review of assessor rationales (2026-10-31): Compound: the heart is the first organ to form, but it is not the origin of the other parts.
- `aristotle-ga02-24`: Verdict changed from true after review of assessor rationales (2026-10-31): Universal over many-toed quadrupeds: spotted hyenas are born with open eyes and many-toed rodents such as guinea pigs are precocial.
- `aristotle-pa04-32`: Verdict changed from true after review of assessor rationales (2026-10-31): Compound: selachians lack opercula, but cartilage is not the cause; chimaeras are cartilaginous and have an opercular flap.
- `aristotle-ha09-27`: excluded. Workers do destroy surplus queen cells, but rival queens do much of the killing, so whether workers kill most queens is disputed.
- `aristotle-ha08-26`: Verdict changed from true after review of assessor rationales (2026-10-31): The claim includes the river crocodile, which does not hide or fast for four winter months; the snake and lizard part holds for temperate species.
- `aristotle-ha09-15`: excluded. Eagles defend territories and young disperse, but other bird species do settle near eagle nests, so the verdict depends on how broadly the last clause is read.

## Worst misses

**GPT-5.6 Sol, attributed**

- `aristotle-ga02-24` (refuted, p_true 0.9): Polydactylous quadrupeds bear blind young whose eyelids open after birth. The named carnivores produce altricial young with sealed eyelids that open after birth, although the broad anatomical category is not a precise causal rule.
- `aristotle-ga02-19` (refuted, p_true 0.85): The heart is the first part to be distinctly formed in all animals with blood. The vertebrate heart and vascular system form very early and the heart is often described as the first functional organ, but 'first distinctly formed in all vertebrates' is stronger than comparative embryology supports.
- `aristotle-pa04-14` (refuted, p_true 0.75): All cephalopods have eight feet, short in cuttlefish and squid, long in octopus. Octopuses have eight long arms, while squid and cuttlefish have eight shorter arms plus two tentacles; the statement captures the arm comparison but is incomplete if all appendages are counted as feet.
- `aristotle-pa04-32` (refuted, p_true 0.65): Selachians lack gill covers because their skeleton is cartilage. Sharks and rays lack the bony operculum typical of most bony fishes, but attributing that absence simply to a cartilaginous skeleton is an unsupported causal explanation.
- `aristotle-ha05-38` (confirmed, p_true 0.55): Ichneumon wasps provision mud-sealed holes with dead spiders. Spider-hunting and mud-nesting wasps do provision sealed nests with spiders, but prey are often paralyzed rather than killed and eggs, not already formed grubs, are laid with them.

**GPT-5.6 Sol, blind**

- `aristotle-ha02-06` (refuted, p_true 0.72): No animal has both tusks and horns, nor does any saw-toothed animal. True horns, tusks and specialized carnassial-like dentitions usually occur in different mammalian lineages, but the universal wording and ambiguous categories make exceptions or definitional counterexamples plausible.
- `aristotle-ha05-02` (confirmed, p_true 0.38): Female cartilaginous fishes are larger than males. Females are larger in many sharks and many fish species, often due to fecundity selection, but male-biased size and little dimorphism are also widespread, so the broad generalization is doubtful.
- `aristotle-ga02-19` (refuted, p_true 0.62): The heart is the first part to be distinctly formed in all animals with blood. The heart and vascular system are among the earliest functioning organs in vertebrate embryos and organize early circulation, but saying it is distinctly first in all blooded animals overstates diverse developmental sequences.
- `aristotle-ha05-27` (confirmed, p_true 0.45): Elephant gestation lasts two years with a single calf. Elephants usually bear one calf, but gestation averages roughly 18 to 22 months rather than uniformly two years, and twins occasionally occur.
- `aristotle-ha05-11` (refuted, p_true 0.45): The halcyon breeds at the winter solstice. Some kingfishers can breed in winter in suitable climates, but breeding timing varies geographically and is not uniquely fixed to the winter solstice.

**Grok 4.7, attributed**

- `aristotle-ha09-26` (confirmed, p_true 0.32): On each trip a bee visits flowers of only one kind. Honey bees show strong flower constancy within a foraging trip, but they do switch plant species before returning, especially when rewards change. The absolute claim that a bee never visits another kind until it is back at the hive does not hold.
- `aristotle-ha02-06` (refuted, p_true 0.6): No animal has both tusks and horns, nor does any saw-toothed animal. Living mammals do not combine true horns with tusks, and carnivores with shearing teeth have neither. A few extinct hoofed mammals carried both large canines and horn-like skull structures, so the absolute version is not airtight.
- `aristotle-pa04-32` (refuted, p_true 0.52): Selachians lack gill covers because their skeleton is cartilage. Most bony fishes have bony opercula, and sharks and rays have open gill slits. Cartilage does not by itself explain the missing cover: other cartilaginous fishes, the chimaeras, have an operculum.
- `aristotle-ga02-24` (refuted, p_true 0.46): Polydactylous quadrupeds bear blind young whose eyelids open after birth. Dogs, lions, wolves, foxes, and jackals are born with sealed eyelids that open days later. Not every many-toed quadruped is altricial in that way: spotted hyena cubs are born with their eyes open.
- `aristotle-ha02-13` (confirmed, p_true 0.58): Crocodiles enter water at night because it is then warmer than the air. At night open water is often warmer than the air because water cools more slowly, and Nile crocodiles spend much time in the water. They also come ashore at night, so the habit is not a strict rule explained only by that temperature difference.

**Grok 4.7, blind**

- `aristotle-ha05-23` (confirmed, p_true 0.4): The bitch's gestation lasts sixty to sixty-three days. Dog pregnancy is usually about 63 days and often falls between 60 and 63 from ovulation, but litters born a little earlier than 60 days from mating can survive.
- `aristotle-ha02-24` (confirmed, p_true 0.45): Horned animals lacking upper front teeth have four stomachs and ruminate. Horned bovids lack upper incisors, have a four-chambered stomach and chew the cud, but camels and chevrotains also ruminate and are not horned animals of that type.
- `aristotle-ha02-13` (confirmed, p_true 0.45): Crocodiles enter water at night because it is then warmer than the air. Crocodiles often stay in water at night, and water can be warmer than night air, but they also come ashore and the thermal explanation is only part of the pattern.
- `aristotle-ha02-06` (refuted, p_true 0.5): No animal has both tusks and horns, nor does any saw-toothed animal. Living horned bovids lack tusks and typical carnivore dentitions lack horns, but deer such as muntjacs combine antlers with elongated canine tusks.
- `aristotle-ha09-26` (confirmed, p_true 0.58): On each trip a bee visits flowers of only one kind. Honey bees show strong flower constancy and often stick to one species on a foraging trip, but mixed trips are common enough that the absolute rule fails.
